## 1. Purpose and intended use

- Estimates, at application time, the probability that an individual unsecured personal loan of the Lending Club type is charged off or defaults over its life (finished loans only).
- Intended use: decision support inside the Underwriting Copilot. The `/score` API returns the PD, a risk band, the top-4 reason codes and scenario (stress) results for an underwriter to review.
- Not for: automated approval or decline, pricing, 12-month PD for IFRS 9 / CECL / Basel capital, or small-business lending. The copilot's target segment would need its own data, re-development and validation.

## 2. Materiality

- Portfolio exposure: not measured (no real portfolio; this is a demonstration).
- Decision impact if it were used: high. Consumer credit decisions carry adverse-action notice requirements (Regulation B) and fair-lending risk.
- Complexity: the LightGBM challenger is less transparent than the scorecard, which is why the champion/challenger rule below prefers the scorecard unless LightGBM is clearly better.
- Proposed tier if deployed: high materiality: independent validation before first use, then at least annual review and the monitoring plan below.

## 4. Methodology

- Champion candidate: logistic-regression scorecard on log-transformed money amounts, values clipped to the 1st-99th training percentile, median imputation with missing-value indicators, standardisation and one-hot categories. Per-feature contributions add up exactly to the log-odds.
- Challenger: LightGBM with class weighting (scale_pos_weight), early stopping on the 2016 validation AUC, and Platt recalibration on 2016 because the weighting inflates probabilities.
- Champion/challenger rule, fixed before the test set is scored: LightGBM only if its 2016 validation AUC beats the scorecard by at least 0.005 and the lower end of the 95% paired-bootstrap interval of the difference is above 0; otherwise the scorecard.
- Selection result: not measured.
- Reason codes: top-4 risk-increasing contributions (SHAP for LightGBM, scorecard contributions otherwise) mapped to plain-language reasons in the style of Regulation B sample reasons.

Reason-code references (cited in the code; applicability depends on the institution):

- Regulation B (Equal Credit Opportunity Act), 12 CFR 1002.9(b)(2) and Appendix C: Adverse action notices must state the specific principal reasons; Appendix C has sample reasons.
- CFPB Circular 2022-03, May 26, 2022: Specific reasons are required even when the credit decision uses a complex algorithm.
- CFPB Circular 2023-03, September 19, 2023: Sample-form reasons cannot be used if they do not reflect the actual principal reasons.

|---|---|---|---|
| Population stability of the PD score | PSI against the training population, per issue-year vintage, then monthly in use | PSI <= 0.10 stable; 0.10 to 0.25 moderate shift (investigate); > 0.25 significant shift (flag) | Flag goes to the model owner; a flag on the score triggers a review of calibration and of the features that moved |
| Population stability of the top-10 features | Same PSI, one per feature | PSI <= 0.10 stable; 0.10 to 0.25 moderate shift (investigate); > 0.25 significant shift (flag) | Explains a score shift; a flagged feature is checked for upstream data changes first |
| Calibration | Observed vs predicted by predicted decile once outcomes mature, with bootstrap 95% CIs | Observed rate outside its CI for several deciles, or total observed / predicted CI excluding 1 | Recalibrate (intercept or Platt) after review; re-validate if the ranking also degrades |

## 10. Limitations

- Finished loans only, with right censoring: loans issued in 2017-2018 had at most about two years to finish before the end of the data, so the finished ones are mostly early payoffs and early charge-offs. Test bad rates and calibration describe that finished subset, not the full 2017-2018 vintages (see vintages).
- Lifetime PD on finished loans, not a 12-month PD (IFRS 9 stage 1 / Basel) and not a through-the-cycle PD.
- Lending Club data covers approved and issued loans only (no reject inference), so the model never sees applicants Lending Club declined.
- Platt recalibration, early stopping and the champion/challenger choice all use the 2016 validation set, which slightly favours LightGBM in that choice (its number of trees was tuned on the same loans); the 2017-2018 test set is used only once, for the reported metrics, and its paired AUC difference is the unbiased check of the choice.
- Column decisions rely on Lending Club's data dictionary; bureau fields cannot be independently verified as point-in-time at application.
- No protected attributes are available in Lending Club data, so fairness cannot be measured here; see the separate fairness_demo section (UCI German Credit). Geography (zip_code, addr_state) is excluded as a proxy.
